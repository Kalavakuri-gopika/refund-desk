# NovaStore E-Commerce Policies & Refund Guidelines

## 1. Return & Refund Window
- Standard return window is **30 calendar days** from the date carrier tracking confirms delivery.
- Items requested outside of 30 days are ineligible for refunds unless an extended holiday policy applies.

## 2. Instant Auto-Refund Threshold ($25.00)
- Any refund request where the order total or requested refund amount is **$25.00 or less** can be auto-approved by the AI assistant without human approval, provided:
  1. The item was delivered within the 30-day return window.
  2. The customer provides a clear reason (e.g., wrong size, minor defect, late delivery, unsatisfied).
  3. The customer has not had more than 2 prior refunds in the last 60 days.
- In these cases, the refund is processed immediately and the customer is not required to ship low-cost items back.

## 3. High-Value Refunds (Over $25.00)
- Any refund exceeding **$25.00** CANNOT be automatically processed by AI.
- The workflow **must pause and request sign-off from the Store Owner / Finance Lead**.
- The request must detail:
  - Order ID, customer email, item description, and purchase amount.
  - Delivery date and days elapsed.
  - Customer stated issue and sentiment score.
- The Store Owner may choose to:
  - Approve full refund.
  - Approve partial refund or store credit.
  - Request return of goods with a prepaid shipping label before releasing funds.
  - Decline the refund with justification.

## 4. Damaged or Defective Items
- Claims of items damaged during shipping or defective on arrival require proof (photo description or carrier damage note).
- Damage claims over $25.00 require Store Owner sign-off before refund or replacement shipment.

## 5. Shipping Delays & Lost Shipments
- If carrier tracking has had no scan event for **5 consecutive business days** while "In Transit", the shipment is classified as potentially lost.
- Customers may be offered a free replacement or full refund after Store Owner verification.
- Routine carrier inquiries ("Where is my order?") should provide the current carrier name, tracking number, and latest tracking status directly from the order records.
